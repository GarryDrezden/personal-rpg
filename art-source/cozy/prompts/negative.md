# Cozy production — negative / forbidden

Never include:

- anime, manga, chibi, Disney, cartoon mascot, mobile farming game, cottagecore Pinterest collage
- neon, acid green, cyber glow, intense purple, black-heavy composition
- gothic, horror, dark fantasy, glowing magic, medieval armor, blood, skulls
- excessive bloom, plastic 3D, luxury mansion, American suburb, futuristic objects
- text, letters, numbers, signage, UI, watermark, logo, brand names
- people, human faces, hero avatars, NPCs
- distorted architecture, extra windows/doors, warped perspective
- pixel art
- kawaii creature faces on household dirt (chapter bosses and daily obstacle cards use metaphors, not mascots)
