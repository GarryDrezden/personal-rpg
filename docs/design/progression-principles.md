# Progression principles

Canonical rules for Personal RPG. New systems must obey these.  
Audit: [`../audits/game-design-consistency-v1.md`](../audits/game-design-consistency-v1.md).

1. **Real behavior first.** The day is logged because it happened — not to farm a currency.
2. **No punishment loops.** A bad day, a gap, or a recovery day must not erase identity or long-term unlocks.
3. **Recovery is valid progress.** Minimal/recovery days hold the route. They may earn less XP; they must not feel like a moral loss.
4. **World reacts to care.** Home, hero chrome, mob/boss presentation answer the day. They are not a second job.
5. **Physical body progression uses physical signals.** Body Stage never shrinks from habits alone. Weight is a signal, not a score, not a fail state.
6. **Meta systems support, not compete.** XP is a receipt. Journey is the life-path. Season is the 28-day rhythm. Momentum is route stability. Hero State is how the hero feels. Achievements are events. Abilities are noticed freedoms.
7. **The player sees a next step, not ten currencies.** Today = action. Dashboard = NOW / NEXT / LONG. Do not teach the full glossary on day one.
8. **Personal paths stay completable.** A −10 kg goal and a −80 kg goal both get a full Journey and Body Stage. Do not hard-gate the campaign on one body.
9. **Theme is presentation.** Cozy and Dark Fantasy share reward mathematics.
10. **No gacha, no FOMO timers, no energy, no premium currency.** Consistency > perfection. The boss is the obstacle, not the player.
